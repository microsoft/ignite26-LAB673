## Drying and the rain

We had **four days of heavy rain** from 15 to 18 June. The greenhouse was full, so about half the lot stayed on open beds under tarps and barely dried for those days. To make the vessel leaving Antsiranana on 26 June, we **bagged the beans one day early**, on 21 June.

Our moisture meter read **7.6%** on a mixed sample at bagging. I must be honest that the beans from the open beds felt softer than the greenhouse beans, so some bags may be wetter than this reading. I recommend you check moisture carefully on arrival.

## Quality

The cut test at the estate (100 beans) showed 85 well fermented, 3 slaty and 2 mouldy. Flavor of a test roast: red berry and citrus, as usual.

We will install the second greenhouse before next year's main crop, as discussed during your visit.
