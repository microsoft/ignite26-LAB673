| Term | Meaning at Cocoarynth |
|---|---|
| **Batch** | One production run of one bar. Its code is printed on the wrapper as the **lot code**, e.g. EC26-0811 (origin, year, month and day). |
| **Bean lot** | A quantity of dried beans shipped and received together, e.g. EC26-L07. One bean lot can feed several batches; one batch can use several lots. |
| **Bloom** | A grey or white film on chocolate. *Fat bloom* comes from warmth; *sugar bloom* from condensation. Safe to eat, but not first quality. |
| **Collection ticket** (*boleta de acopio*) | The paper slip written when a farmer delivers fresh beans to a cooperative. Shows farm, weight and lot. |
| **Cut test** | Cutting 300 beans in half to check fermentation and defects. |
| **Hold** | A bean lot that cannot be used until Quality resolves a problem, such as high moisture. |
| **Landed cost** | What we pay per kg of beans delivered to our factory, including freight and import. |
| **Mouldy bean** | A bean with visible mould inside when cut. |
| **Release** | Quality's sign-off that a bean lot or batch may be used or sold. |
| **Sample match** | Contract clause letting us reject a lot that does not taste like the approved sample. |
| **Sell-in** | Bars we sell to retailers (not what retailers sell to shoppers). |
| **Single origin** | All cocoa from one region and supplier — usually several farms. |
| **Slaty bean** | A grey, unfermented bean when cut; tastes bitter and astringent. |
| **Transaction certificate** | A certifier's document proving a specific shipment is organic. |
| **Wrapper lot code** | The batch code printed on the back of every bar. |
