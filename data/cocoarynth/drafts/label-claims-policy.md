## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out what Cocoarynth may print on wrappers, shelf cards, product pages and marketing. Every claim must be true, provable from our records and approved before printing.

## 2. Who approves

Quality approves every wrapper proof before it is printed or applied. Sales may not add claims to retailer materials that are not on the approved wrapper.

## 3. Origin claims

3.1 **Single origin.** A bar may be called single-origin only if all of its cocoa comes from one country and one growing region, bought from one supplier. A single-origin bar may combine beans from several farms in that region.

3.2 **Farm names.** A wrapper or shelf card may name farms only if it names **every** farm that contributed beans to that batch.

3.3 **Harvest year** is the year the beans were harvested, not the year the bar was made.

## 4. Organic claims

4.1 A bar may carry the **organic seal** or the word "organic" **only if both** of the following are true:

- the supplier's organic certificate is **valid on the packing date** of the batch; and
- a **transaction certificate** covering the bean lot used in the batch is on file with Quality.

4.2 If the supplier's certificate has **expired, is suspended or is pending renewal** on the packing date, the batch must be packed **without** the organic seal and must not be described as organic, even if the beans were harvested while the certificate was valid.

4.3 Organic sugar alone does not allow an organic claim for the bar.

## 5. Other claims

| Claim | Allowed when |
|---|---|
| "Direct trade" | We buy from the supplier without a trader, other than our importer of record |
| Cocoa percentage | Matches the recipe for that batch |
| "Fine flavor" | The origin scored 7.0 or more at our latest sensory panel |
| Health claims (antioxidants, "superfood", etc.) | **Never** |

## 6. Records

Quality keeps wrapper proofs, certificates and transaction certificates for five years.
