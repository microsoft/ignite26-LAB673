## Purpose

Each year Sourcing compares what we pay for beans with the world commodity reference and asks whether each premium still makes sense. All prices are **landed cost**: what we pay the supplier plus export, freight, import and handling to our Portland factory, per kg of dried beans.

Our premium over the reference is now between **USD 4.20/kg (Ghana)** and **USD 8.60/kg (Madagascar)**. **Madagascar's Tsaravola Estate is our most expensive origin at USD 13.80/kg landed.**

## Question for leadership

With the world reference down, should we keep paying the Tsaravola estate price? Sourcing's view is that the premium buys a distinctive flavor, but the answer depends on how the bar performs with tasters and customers. Please review alongside the sensory panel report and the Q3 sales review.
