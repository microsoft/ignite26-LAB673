The **Cooperativa Agraria Nuevo Horizonte** brings together 212 families growing native Amazonian cacao around Bagua, in Peru's Amazonas region, between 450 and 700 meters above sea level. We have bought from them since 2020.

{{ photo("solar-dryer-peru", "Solar dryer with raised mesh beds at the cooperative's central fermentary.") }}

## Centralized processing

Members deliver fresh beans in pulp to the cooperative's central fermentary, where quality officer **Lucía Huamán** oversees:

- **Fermentation** in cascading wooden boxes for six to seven days.
- **Drying** in tunnel solar dryers on raised mesh beds for nine to eleven days.

Central processing gives very consistent lots, which is why the Peru bar tastes so similar from batch to batch.

## Organic certification

Nuevo Horizonte is certified organic (USDA NOP and EU) by CertiOrgánica Andina under certificate **CA-ORG-2025-0381**, valid until **31 May 2026**. The annual renewal inspection is scheduled for April 2026. Our contract pays an organic premium of USD 0.40/kg while the certificate is valid.

Citrus, honey and yellow fruit. These beans make **Peru Bagua 70%**.
