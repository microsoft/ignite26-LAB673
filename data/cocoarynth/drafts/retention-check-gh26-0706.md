## Why this check

On 18 July 2026 Larkspur Market reported that Ghana Akwapim Ridge 68% bars from batch **GH26-0706** looked grey and dusty (case CASE-2607-18). Before answering the retailer, Quality checked whether the batch itself was faulty.

## Retained bars

All **10 retained bars** from GH26-0706, kept since 7 July in the retention sample room at 18 °C, were unwrapped and examined on **20 July 2026**.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Surface | Glossy, even, dark brown — **no bloom** on any bar |
| Snap | Clean, sharp snap on all 10 |
| Melt and flavor | Normal; classic cocoa and roasted nut |
| Temper meter (re-melt test) | Not needed |

## Conclusion

GH26-0706 **left the factory in good condition**. The problem appears limited to the Larkspur delivery, so the cause is likely something that happened to that shipment in transit. Logistics to investigate the route.
