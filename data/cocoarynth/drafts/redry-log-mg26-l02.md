## Setup

Lot **MG26-L02** (420.0 kg, on hold H-2607-02) was spread in shallow perforated trays on steel racks in the bean conditioning room from **3 to 14 August 2026**. Room held at 22–24 °C and 40–45% relative humidity with the dehumidifier running continuously. Beans were stirred twice a day.
