## Section 1 — Nonconformance (15 July 2026)

**Description.** Trial lot DR26-T02 rejected: smoky, hammy off-flavor detected by 4 of 5 tasters; not present in the approved sample DR26-T01.

**Disposition.** Lot rejected and quarantined. Under the sample-match clause the supplier credited the full lot. Beans composted on 22 July 2026 (not blended, not used for food).

**Request to supplier.** Investigate the source of the smoke and confirm corrective action before any further shipment.

## Section 2 — Supplier response (received 12 August 2026)

The association's field technician, Yanelis Peña, replied that her field visit report of 20 May had already noted a problem with the drying setup at Finca El Guayabo. The farm has now:

- **moved its drying racks about 30 m upwind** of the farm's dryer, and
- **extended the dryer chimney above the roofline**.

Corrective action completed on **12 August 2026**. Photos attached.

## Section 3 — Verification and closure (18 September 2026)

A 2 kg follow-up sample, **DR26-T03**, dried on the relocated racks, arrived on 15 September and passed moisture (7.0%) and sensory on 16 September with **no smoke detected by any taster**. NCR closed.

Owner: Marcus Bell, Quality Manager.
