This specification is part of every Cocoarynth supply contract.

## 1. Product

Dried, fermented cocoa beans (*Theobroma cacao*) of the agreed origin, packed in new, clean jute bags of about 60 kg, each marked with the lot code.

## 2. Quality limits

| Parameter | Limit |
|---|---|
| Moisture | 7.5% maximum on arrival |
| Well fermented (cut test, 300 beans) | 75% minimum target |
| Mouldy | 3% maximum |
| Slaty | 5% maximum |
| Insect-damaged | 3% maximum |
| Foreign matter | 1% maximum |
| Off-flavors | None (smoky, hammy, musty, chemical) |
| Microbiology | Salmonella not detected in 25 g; yeast and mould ≤ 1 × 10⁴ CFU/g |

## 3. Sample match

Before each new supplier or new season, the supplier sends a **2 kg pre-shipment sample**. If a shipped lot **does not match the approved sample** in flavor, Cocoarynth may **reject it with full credit** to Cocoarynth. The supplier is responsible for disposal or return costs.

## 4. Good drying practice

- Dry beans on raised beds, mats or patios — never directly on the ground.
- **Keep drying beans away from smoke**, exhaust, fuel and chemicals. Wood or fuel-fired dryers must vent their smoke well away from and above the drying area.
- Cover beans during rain and do not bag them until they are fully dry.

## 5. Records

The supplier keeps collection tickets, fermentation and drying records for each lot and shares them on request.
