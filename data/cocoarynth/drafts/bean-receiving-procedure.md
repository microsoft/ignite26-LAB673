## 1. Purpose

Every lot of cocoa beans must be checked and released by Quality before Production may use it. This procedure sets the checks, limits and decisions.

## 2. Receiving steps

1. **Weigh and inspect.** Logistics weighs every bag and notes damage, wet or stained jute, odors and pests on the receiving note.
2. **Sample.** Quality draws a composite sample from every bag (0.5–1 kg in total, booked as a deduction from the lot).
3. **Moisture.** Measure with the calibrated moisture meter (% wet basis).
4. **Cut test.** Cut **300 beans** lengthwise and classify each one: well fermented, partly fermented, slaty, purple, mouldy, insect-damaged, germinated or flat.
5. **Sensory check.** Bench-roast and taste with at least **three tasters**.
6. **Decide:** release, hold or reject (section 4).

## 3. Limits

| Check | Limit |
|---|---|
| Moisture | **7.5% maximum** |
| Mouldy beans | 3% maximum (9 of 300) |
| Slaty beans | 5% maximum (15 of 300) |
| Insect-damaged beans | 3% maximum (9 of 300) |
| Off-flavors | Any smoky, hammy or musty off-flavor confirmed by **at least three tasters** rejects the lot |

## 4. Decisions

- **Release:** all checks pass. Quality signs the lot release record; only then can Production issue the beans.
- **Hold:** the lot is over the moisture limit but otherwise acceptable. Mark the bags HOLD, move them to the bean conditioning room and **re-dry** in shallow trays with dehumidification. Retest moisture (and send a sample to an independent lab for moisture and microbiology). Release when moisture is at or below 7.5%.
- **Reject:** cut-test limits exceeded or an off-flavor confirmed. Raise a nonconformance report, notify Sourcing, and apply the supplier contract (sample-match clause). Rejected beans are never blended into other lots.

## 5. Records

Receiving note (Logistics), receiving register, hold notice, conditioning-room log, lab certificate, lot release record, nonconformance report.
