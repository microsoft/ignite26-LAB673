This supply agreement is made on 1 December 2025 between **Cocoarynth Chocolate Works** ("Cocoarynth") and **Larkspur Market** ("Retailer"), a regional grocer with six stores in eastern Washington and northern Idaho.

## 1. Products and prices

1.1 Cocoarynth will supply the bars listed in its current wholesale price list. For 2026 the Retailer will stock Ecuador Río Claro 72%, Ghana Akwapim Ridge 68% and Peru Bagua 70%.

1.2 Prices are those in the wholesale price list in force on the order date. Payment terms are 30 days from invoice.

## 2. Orders and delivery

2.1 The Retailer orders at least 10 days before the requested delivery date, in full cases of 24 bars.

2.2 Cocoarynth delivers to the Retailer's Spokane distribution center using a carrier of its choice. Risk passes to the Retailer on signed delivery.

## 3. Storage and handling

3.1 Both parties will keep the products at **15–20 °C**, below 60% relative humidity and away from strong odors, **in transit, in storage and on display**.

3.2 Products must not be displayed in direct sunlight or next to heat sources.

## 4. Quality issues and credits

4.1 The Retailer reports any quality issue within 7 days of delivery, with photos and the wrapper lot code.

4.2 If Cocoarynth confirms the issue was caused before delivery (including in transit), it will credit or replace the affected bars. Bars damaged by storage at the Retailer's premises are not credited.

## 5. Traceability information

5.1 Cocoarynth will provide, for shelf labels and customer questions: **origin country and region, supplier and farm names, cocoa percentage and batch (lot) code**.

5.2 The Retailer will not publish farmer names, contact details or prices paid to suppliers.

## 6. Term

This agreement runs from 1 January to 31 December 2026 and renews automatically for one year unless either party gives 60 days' notice.
