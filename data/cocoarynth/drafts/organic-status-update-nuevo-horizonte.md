**To:** Marcus Bell (Quality), Lin Zhou (Production), Talia Brooks (Sales)

## Summary

The organic certificate of our Peru supplier, **Cooperativa Agraria Nuevo Horizonte**, is **not currently valid**. Certificate **CA-ORG-2025-0381** expired on **31 May 2026** and the renewal is **still pending**.

## Where things stand

- CertiOrgánica Andina carried out the annual renewal inspection on 22 April 2026 and withheld renewal until a major finding is closed (see the inspection report on file).
- The cooperative planted a 10 m living hedge of Inga seedlings along the affected boundary and submitted photo evidence on **28 August 2026**.
- The certifier's follow-up visit is booked for **20 October 2026**. A renewal decision is expected a few weeks after that visit.

{{ photo("inga-hedge-planting", "Evidence submitted by the cooperative on 28 August: new Inga hedge along the east boundary of Chacra Los Laureles.") }}

## What this means for us

1. **Contract:** the USD 0.40/kg organic premium in contract CT-NH-2026 is suspended for any beans shipped while the certificate is not valid.
2. **Labels:** Production should apply the label-claims policy (POL-03) to every Peru batch packed after 31 May, and check with Quality before printing wrappers.
3. **Sales:** please do not describe current Peru stock as organic in retailer communications until we confirm renewal.

I will circulate the certifier's decision as soon as we receive it.
