**Re: Shipment SHP-2607-031, pallet LM-P02 — incident response**

Dear Sam,

Thank you for raising the issue with pallet LM-P02, delivered to Larkspur Market on 15 July. We have reviewed the shipment with our Riverbend depot team.

**What happened.** The pallet arrived at our Riverbend depot in Pasco at 07:30 on 14 July. Its onward truck was rescheduled to the evening, and Cold Room 2 was full with a produce consignment. Contrary to your handling instructions, the depot supervisor staged the pallet in **Overflow Bay 4**, which is not temperature controlled, from about 08:00 until it was loaded at 20:10 — roughly **12 hours**. A heat advisory was in effect for the Tri-Cities that day.

**What we are changing.**

1. Cocoarynth pallets will **never be staged in overflow bays** at any Northwind depot.
2. If a cold room is full, the depot supervisor will **call Cocoarynth Logistics before moving the pallet** and will hold it in a refrigerated trailer instead.
3. All Riverbend staff will receive refresher training on temperature-sensitive cargo by 15 August.

We apologize for this lapse and will discuss compensation with your accounts team.

Yours sincerely,

Karen Holt
Customer Operations Manager, Northwind Cold Freight
