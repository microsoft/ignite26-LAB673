## 1. Purpose

Customers and retailers often ask where their chocolate comes from. We want to answer generously while protecting the privacy and safety of the farmers we work with and our commercial terms. These rules apply to everyone who asks: customers, retailers and the public.

## 2. What we may share

| Information | Shareable? |
|---|---|
| Origin country and region | Yes |
| Supplier (cooperative, association or estate) name | Yes |
| **Farm names** | **Yes** |
| Harvest year | Yes |
| Batch (wrapper lot) code and bean lot code | Yes |
| Fermentation and drying methods, flavor notes | Yes |
| **Farmer personal names** | **No** |
| Farmer phone numbers, email or home addresses | **No** |
| GPS coordinates or maps precise enough to locate a farm | **No** |
| Prices paid to individual suppliers or farmers | **No** (only aggregate averages in our public origin report) |

## 3. Answering "which farm grew my bar?"

1. Get the **lot code** from the back of the wrapper (a photo is fine).
2. Find the batch record for that lot code and note the **bean lot(s)** used.
3. Look up the bean lot in the origin records (collection tickets or supplier lot records) to find the **contributing farms**.
4. Reply with the **farm names**, region and supplier. If several farms contributed, name them all.
5. Do **not** include farmer names or contact details.
