## Why we publish this report

Every Cocoarynth bar is made from two ingredients: cocoa beans and cane sugar. That makes the beans — and the people who grow, ferment and dry them — everything. This report shares where our cacao comes from, how it is processed and what we pay for it compared with the world market.

## Our origins

### Esmeraldas, Ecuador — Cooperativa Agroartesanal Río Claro

86 families on Ecuador's northern coast. We buy mostly from Finca Las Brisas, Finca El Cedro and Finca La Ceiba. Beans are fermented in tiered boxes and dried on raised beds at the cooperative's collection center. Floral, jasmine and dried fig.

{{ photo("cacao-pods-ecuador", "Ripening pods in Esmeraldas.", "half") }}

### Akwapim Ridge, Ghana — Akwapim Ridge Cocoa Farmers Association

140 farmers in the hills of the Eastern Region, including Nyamebekyere, Adom and Asaase farms. Farmers ferment in heaps under banana leaves and dry on raised bamboo mats paid for with our community premium. Classic cocoa and roasted nut.

{{ photo("heap-fermentation-ghana", "Heap fermentation in Akwapim.", "half") }}

### Bagua, Peru — Cooperativa Agraria Nuevo Horizonte

212 families growing native Amazonian cacao, including Chacra San Isidro and Chacra Los Laureles. Central fermentary and solar dryers. Citrus and honey.

### Sambirano Valley, Madagascar — Tsaravola Estate

A single estate with two parcels that grows, ferments and dries its own beans. Red berry and citrus zest.

### Hato Mayor, Dominican Republic — Asociación Loma Verde (trial)

A new partnership we are developing with a 38-member association. We are working with farmers on drying practices and hope to launch a Dominican bar in 2027.

## What we pay

{{ chart("landed-price-by-origin", "Average landed price per kg by origin compared with the world reference.") }}

In 2026 we paid an average of **USD 9.40 to 13.80 per kg** landed, against a world reference of **USD 5.20**. Premiums reward fermentation and drying quality and fund community projects such as drying frames.

## Glossary

- **Landed cost:** what we pay per kg, including freight and import, delivered to our factory.
- **Single origin:** all the cacao in a bar comes from one region and supplier — often several farms.
- **Fermentation:** the few days when pulp-covered beans develop the precursors of chocolate flavor.
