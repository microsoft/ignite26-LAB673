## Method

Our annual panel tasted every single-origin bar on **22 September 2026**. Nine trained panelists from Quality, Production and Sales tasted 5 g pieces of each 70 g bar at 20 °C in individual booths. Samples carried random three-digit codes, so no one knew which origin they were tasting. Each panelist scored seven flavor attributes from 0 (absent) to 10 (very strong), checked for off-flavors, and gave an overall quality score from 0 to 10.

## Discussion

**Madagascar Tsaravola 70% was the clear winner**, with an overall score of **8.4**. Seven of nine panelists ranked it first. Panelists described **red berry and raspberry, citrus zest, bright but balanced acidity and low bitterness**. It had the highest fruit score of any bar (8.5) and one of the lowest bitterness scores (3.0).

Ecuador Río Claro 72% came second (7.9) with the strongest floral character. Ghana Akwapim Ridge 68% scored lower overall (7.2) but was voted the most "chocolatey" bar, with the highest cocoa (8.0) and nutty (6.0) scores.

The Dominican bench bar, made from follow-up sample DR26-T03, scored 7.0 with **no off-flavors** — a big improvement on the smoky trial lot in July. The panel recommends continuing the trial.

— Devi Narayan, Sensory Panel Coordinator
