## What we believe

Our bars have two ingredients, so flavor starts on the farm. We buy beans for their flavor and the care that goes into them, and we pay for that care.

## Our principles

1. **Pay for quality, not volume.** We pay well above the world commodity price, and we add bonuses when fermentation and drying are excellent.
2. **Know the farms.** We buy from cooperatives, associations and estates that can tell us which farms contributed to each lot.
3. **Stay for the long term.** We aim to buy from each partner year after year, so they can plan and invest.
4. **Invest together.** Premiums fund practical improvements: drying frames, greenhouse dryers, hedges and training.
5. **Be honest about claims.** We only make certification claims we can prove on the day a bar is packed.
6. **Be transparent.** We publish our average landed prices against the world reference every year.

## How we judge whether a premium is worth it

A higher price for beans is justified when:

- the beans give a **distinctive, high-scoring flavor** at our blind sensory panel; **and**
- customers and retailers are **willing to pay more** for the bar, so the premium is sustainable for us as well as the grower.

If an origin costs more but does not stand out in tasting or sales, we work with the supplier on quality before we reconsider the relationship.
