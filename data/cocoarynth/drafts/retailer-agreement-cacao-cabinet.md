This supply agreement is made on 15 November 2025 between **Cocoarynth Chocolate Works** ("Cocoarynth") and **The Cacao Cabinet** ("Retailer"), a specialty chocolate shop in San Francisco owned by Mei Lindqvist.

## 1. Products and prices

1.1 The Retailer may stock any Cocoarynth bar. For 2026 it stocks Ecuador Río Claro 72% and 85%, Peru Bagua 70% and Madagascar Tsaravola 70%.

1.2 Wholesale prices follow the current price list. The Retailer receives a 5% discount on orders above 500 bars.

## 2. Limited editions

2.1 The Retailer receives **first allocation of every limited edition**, delivered before the public launch date so it can be on shelf on launch day.

2.2 Cocoarynth will give at least four weeks' notice of limited-edition launch dates and any change to them.

## 3. Storage and handling

3.1 Products are delivered direct by refrigerated truck and stored by the Retailer at 15–20 °C.

## 4. Shelf cards and traceability

4.1 Cocoarynth will provide shelf-card content for each bar, including **origin, supplier and farm names**, harvest year and tasting notes.

4.2 Shelf cards must **not** include farmer names, contact details, GPS locations or prices paid to suppliers.

## 5. Term

1 January to 31 December 2026, renewing annually unless either party gives 60 days' notice.

Signed for Cocoarynth: Talia Brooks · Signed for The Cacao Cabinet: Mei Lindqvist, Owner
