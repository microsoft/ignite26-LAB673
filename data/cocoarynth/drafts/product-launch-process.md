## Purpose

New bars and limited editions involve Sourcing, Quality, Production and Sales. This procedure sets the gates every launch must pass.

## Stage gates

| Gate | Owner | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Concept | Sales | Product idea, target retailers and launch date agreed |
| 2. Recipe | Production and Quality | Bench recipe approved at a sensory session |
| 3. **Released beans** | **Quality** | **The bean lot for the run is released under QP-04** |
| 4. Production | Production | Batch made, packed and released by Quality |
| 5. Packaging and claims | Quality | Wrapper proof approved under POL-03 |
| 6. Retailer notice | Sales | Retailers told at least **four weeks** before launch |
| 7. Launch | Sales | Stock delivered to first-allocation retailers before launch day |

## Rules

- **Production may not start until the bean lot is released by Quality.** A lot on hold cannot be used, even for a trial run.
- If any gate slips, Sales decides with Production whether to move the launch date and tells retailers within two working days.
- Limited editions launch on the first day of a month.
