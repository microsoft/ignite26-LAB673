## Sample

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Lot | DR26-T02, trial lot, Asociación Loma Verde, Dominican Republic |
| Farm | Finca El Guayabo |
| Received | 8 July 2026, 6 bags, 359.0 kg |
| Moisture / cut test | 6.9% / pass (240 of 300 well fermented) |
| Reference | Approved sample DR26-T01 (10 March 2026) |
| Tasting date | 13 July 2026 |

## Method

Bench roast at 125 °C for 22 minutes; beans cracked, winnowed and ground into liquor. Tasted blind alongside retained DR26-T01 sample liquor by five tasters.

## Results

| Taster | Smoky / hammy off-flavor? | Comments |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yes | Campfire on the nose, smoked ham on the finish |
| 2 | Yes | Strong smoke, drying |
| 3 | Yes | Smoky, "like bacon" |
| 4 | No | Earthy, slightly flat |
| 5 | Yes | Smoke dominates the molasses |

**4 of 5 tasters** detected a **smoky, hammy off-flavor** that was absent from the approved sample DR26-T01. Molasses and raisin notes are still present underneath.

## Recommendation

The lot fails the off-flavor rule in QP-04 (three or more tasters). **Reject DR26-T02** under the sample-match clause in contract CT-LV-2026 and ask the association to find the source of the smoke before any further shipment.

— Devi Narayan, Sensory Panel Coordinator
