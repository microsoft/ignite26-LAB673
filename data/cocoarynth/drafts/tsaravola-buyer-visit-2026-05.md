## Purpose

Annual buyer visit to Tsaravola Estate, Sambirano Valley, 10–13 May 2026, ahead of the June harvest. Hosted by estate manager Hery Rakotomalala.

## What I saw

- **Parcels:** both the River and Hillside parcels look healthy; pod load is good after a wet start to the year. Pruning on the Hillside parcel is overdue but planned for July.
- **Fermentary:** four-tier boxes in good repair; the estate now records box temperatures twice daily.
- **Drying:** the greenhouse dryer works well, but it holds only about **half of a peak-week harvest**. The rest dries on open raised beds that have to be covered by hand when it rains. In a wet week this slows drying and risks beans being bagged before they are fully dry.

{{ photo("greenhouse-drying-rain", "Greenhouse dryer (left) and open beds covered with tarps during a shower.") }}

## Agreements

1. The estate will add a **second greenhouse dryer before the 2027 main crop** (target 30 April 2027). We discussed a co-investment; I will bring a proposal to the leadership team.
2. For the June shipment, the estate will message us the moisture reading at bagging.
3. Pricing for 2026 stays at the agreed estate price.
