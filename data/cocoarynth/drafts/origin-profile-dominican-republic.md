## Why a fifth origin?

Our four current origins cover floral (Ecuador), classic cocoa (Ghana), citrus and honey (Peru) and red fruit (Madagascar). We have no bar with the deep molasses and dried-fruit character of Hispaniola cacao. In 2026 we are trialing beans from the Dominican Republic to fill that gap.

## The partner

The **Asociación de Productores de Cacao Loma Verde** groups 38 farmers in Hato Mayor province. Unlike our larger partners, each farm ferments in its own wooden boxes and dries on its own racks and patios; the association's field technician, **Yanelis Peña**, visits farms and pools lots for export.

Two farms are part of the trial:

- **Finca Loma Verde** (Ramón Castillo), 6.0 ha under coconut and Gliricidia.
- **Finca El Guayabo** (Miguel Reyes), 4.5 ha under guava, banana and Gliricidia.

## Sample results

A 2 kg sample from Finca Loma Verde (**DR26-T01**) arrived on 4 March at 6.9% moisture. Our sensory panel tasted it on **10 March 2026** and approved it: molasses, raisin and a clean finish.

## Next step

We have agreed a **360 kg trial lot from Finca El Guayabo** at USD 10.10/kg landed. The contract includes our standard **sample-match clause**: if the trial lot does not taste like the approved sample, we may reject it with full credit. If the lot performs, a Dominican bar could launch in 2027.
