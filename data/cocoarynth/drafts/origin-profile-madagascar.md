**Tsaravola Estate** sits in the Sambirano Valley in northwestern Madagascar, a river valley famous for bright, fruity cacao. Unlike our cooperative partners, Tsaravola is a single estate: it grows, ferments, dries and exports its own beans. Estate manager **Hery Rakotomalala** has supplied Cocoarynth since 2021.

## The estate

| Parcel | Planted area | Shade |
|---|---|---|
| Tsaravola River Parcel | 18.0 ha | Albizia and jackfruit |
| Tsaravola Hillside Parcel | 11.5 ha | Albizia and mango |

Lots usually combine beans from both parcels.

## Post-harvest

- **Fermentation:** four-tier wooden boxes for six days, turned daily after day two.
- **Drying:** raised beds, with a greenhouse cover used in the rainy season; seven to twelve days depending on weather.

{{ photo("greenhouse-drying-rain", "The estate's greenhouse dryer and open beds.") }}

Beans travel by road to Antsiranana and by sea to Oakland, usually transshipped at Port Louis. The estate ships to us **twice a year**.

## Why we pay more

Tsaravola beans are the most expensive we buy. The estate price covers on-site fermentation and drying, careful sorting and very small, separate lots. In return we get the distinctive **red berry, citrus zest and bright acidity** that define **Madagascar Tsaravola 70%**.

## Certification

Tsaravola is **not certified organic**. Our wrappers and marketing must not imply that it is.
