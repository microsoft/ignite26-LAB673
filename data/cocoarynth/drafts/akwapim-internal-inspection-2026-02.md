## Scope

Annual internal inspection of the three member farms that supply most of the association's specialty lots: Nyamebekyere Farm, Adom Farm and Asaase Farm. Inspector: Kwabena Asare, association secretary, accompanied by two lead farmers. Visits took place on 23 and 24 February 2026.

## Checklist summary

| Area | Nyamebekyere | Adom | Asaase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ripe pods only harvested | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Heap fermentation 6 days with 2 turns | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Drying off the ground | Yes | Yes | **No** |
| Storage away from fuel and chemicals | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Child labor risk check | Clear | Clear | Clear |
