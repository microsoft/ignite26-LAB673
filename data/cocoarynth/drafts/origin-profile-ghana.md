Our Ghana beans come from the **Akwapim Ridge Cocoa Farmers Association** in the hills of the Eastern Region, about an hour north of Accra. The association has 140 member farmers; its secretary, **Kwabena Asare**, coordinates quality checks, pooling and export.

{{ photo("heap-fermentation-ghana", "Heap fermentation under banana leaves on a member farm.") }}

## How the beans are processed

Unlike our Latin American partners, Akwapim Ridge members ferment and dry their own beans on the farm and deliver them dry to the association:

- **Fermentation:** beans are piled in heaps on banana leaves, covered, and turned twice over six days.
- **Drying:** raised bamboo mats in full sun for seven to nine days.

In 2025 we began paying a **community premium of USD 0.25/kg** that the association uses to buy bamboo frames so every member can dry beans off the ground.

## Flavor

Classic, deep cocoa with roasted nut and brown bread notes — the most "chocolatey" of our origins. These beans make **Ghana Akwapim Ridge 68%**.
