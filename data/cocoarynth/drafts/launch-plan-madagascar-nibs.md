**To:** Sales team; buyers at The Cacao Cabinet, Pantry Lane and Hoshizora Foods

## Change

The launch of our limited edition **Madagascar Tsaravola 70% with Cacao Nibs** moves from **1 September 2026** to **1 October 2026**.

## Why

The beans for this run arrived on 28 July and are on a **quality hold** in our factory. Production cannot start until Quality releases the lot, and our launch process needs at least four weeks between packing and launch to get stock to retailers. We expect the beans to be released in mid to late August.

## Revised timeline

| Step | Original plan | New plan |
|---|---|---|
| Production start | 30 July | Late August (after bean release) |
| Packed and released | 1 August | Early September |
| Retailer stock delivered | 25 August | Late September |
| **Public launch** | **1 September** | **1 October** |

## What we're telling retailers

- The Cacao Cabinet keeps its first allocation (360 bars), delivered before 1 October.
- Pantry Lane will list the product page as "coming 1 October".
- Hoshizora Foods will add the bar to its Q4 import order.

The core Madagascar Tsaravola 70% bar is not affected; it is made from a different bean lot.

— Talia Brooks, Sales and Partnerships Lead
