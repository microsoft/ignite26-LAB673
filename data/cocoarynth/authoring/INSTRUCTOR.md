Keep this file out of participant document ingestion. This is a proposed interpretation of authored investigation evidence, not an approved operational finding or proof of product safety.

## Leading explanation

Temperature excursions during Oceanbridge overflow staging, followed by cooling, are the leading shipment-related explanation for the delayed whitening and apparent texture/shape changes. The pattern is compatible with fat bloom from cocoa-butter crystal disruption. The deposit is not analytically identified; ambient readings do not prove a wrapped bar melted. Poor initial tempering, condensation/sugar bloom and unobserved later storage conditions remain possible.

## What a defensible solution contains

A strong analysis names the shared staging exposure as the leading hypothesis, supports it through multilingual multi-hop joins and matched controls, and proposes a controlled QA investigation plus review of conditioned staging and monitoring. It does not announce proven melting, microbial spoilage, safe consumption, automatic release or an already-approved corrective action.

Further work would need appropriate deposit/crystal identification, product-temperature and exposure-duration evidence, more representative matched sampling and a review of condensation and unobserved storage intervals. Temperature logging and a controlled comparison should distinguish ambient from product-core placement. Actual disposition and safety decisions require responsible QA review; no tasting or consumption exercise is part of these materials.
